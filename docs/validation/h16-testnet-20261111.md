The methods now use pointer receivers and the redundant disconnect callback read is removed. A dedicated race regression reproduced the defect before the fix and passed afterwards. Final mining/disconnect requalification is recorded in the pull request; the earlier mining counts do not conceal that intermediate failure.

## Limits

It does not execute a paid private request, audit/attention cycle, reward recovery, mainnet deployment, remote TLS, sustained mixed workload or multi-worker pool load. Crypto response roundtrips and tampering refusals are local Rust tests, not on-chain inference acceptance.

No personal wallet or mainnet funds were spent. Test-only rewards were not claimed. Test processes were bounded, owned separately, and stopped after evidence collection; raw logs and secret state are excluded from this repository.
